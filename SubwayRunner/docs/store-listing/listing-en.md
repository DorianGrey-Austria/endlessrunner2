## Description (long)

Run, jump and duck through 10 different worlds — from a nighttime subway tunnel to
a neon city. Subway Runner 3D is a fast-paced endless runner with real 3D graphics
rendered right on your device.

SIGNATURE FEATURE: HEAD CONTROL
Play hands-free using head movements via your camera — tilt your head to change
lanes, nod to jump. All camera processing happens entirely on your device; nothing
is ever recorded or transmitted.

FEATURES
- 10 worlds with rising difficulty and unique obstacles
- Precise swipe controls, keyboard, or head/body motion control
- 5 selectable characters
- Multi-jump system: up to 4 jumps mid-air
- 6 music tracks from synthwave to lo-fi
- Local leaderboard — beat your own best

PRIVACY FIRST
No accounts, no tracking, no hidden data collection. Your progress stays on your
device.

Can you beat all 10 worlds?
